What to confirm before agreeing to work

A plumbing visit may involve identifying the source of a problem before deciding what should be done. Ask what the plumber plans to examine and how they will describe the recommended work. If you don’t understand a term or why a step is needed, ask for a simpler explanation before you approve anything.

Use this hiring checklist:
- Give a concise account of the symptoms and when they occur.
- Ask whether the company handles this kind of plumbing job.
- Find out what access or preparation may be needed.
- Confirm the timing and who to contact if plans change.
- Request an explanation of the proposed work and related costs before authorizing it.

Avoid choosing based only on a broad claim or assuming the lowest quote includes everything you need. Make sure you understand what is included, what may require a separate decision, and how the plumber will communicate changes. If a situation seems urgent, say so when you call and ask what options are available.
