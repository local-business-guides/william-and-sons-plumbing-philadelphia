How to prepare for a repair visit

The repair process depends on what the plumber finds, so keep your notes factual. Describe any visible signs, the location, when the issue occurs, and any change you’ve noticed over time. If the problem is intermittent, explain how often it appears and what you were doing when you noticed it. Don’t cover up the affected area before asking whether it needs to be seen.

Before the conversation, consider this checklist:

  • Identify the room, fixture, or area involved.
  • Note when the problem began and whether it has changed.
  • Mention any steps you’ve already taken, even if they didn’t help.
  • Keep the path to the affected spot accessible if a visit is arranged.
  • Ask what the plumber recommends and confirm the scope before work begins.

These details can help the plumber assess what to inspect. They don’t replace an on-site assessment or establish a diagnosis on their own.
