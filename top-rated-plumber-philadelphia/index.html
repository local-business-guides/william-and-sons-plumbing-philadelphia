What to ask before plumbing work

The work depends on the problem, so don’t assume every plumbing call follows the same plan. Start by describing the issue and asking what the plumber will check. If a repair is recommended, ask what it addresses and whether there are other reasonable options. Before approving work, make sure you understand its scope and any decisions you need to make.

A quick checklist can keep the conversation clear:

  • Note where the issue is happening and when you first noticed it.
  • Share any changes, such as a new sound, smell or visible leak.
  • Ask what the plumber plans to inspect and explain.
  • Confirm the next step before work begins.
  • Keep the plumber’s contact details handy if you need to follow up.

Avoid guessing at the cause or trying a repair you’re not comfortable doing. A rushed assumption can make it harder to explain what happened. If water is actively causing damage or the problem is getting worse, contact a plumber promptly and describe the situation clearly.
